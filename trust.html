How verification works

A green badge is a claim. Here's the proof behind it.

"Verified" on a listing means our team checked it — this page explains who does that checking, what protects your money while a purchase is pending, and how to reach us directly, not just through the website.

What we check before a listing shows "Verified"

1

Ownership matches the deed holder's name

We cross-check the registered deed against the name of whoever is listing the property — not just a copy they've sent us.

2

Mutation record confirmed at the local land office

Deeds change hands; mutation records prove the current government record actually reflects that. We confirm this directly with the district land office, not from a photo the seller sends us.

3

Litigation check

We check for pending disputes or court cases against the plot as of the check date. This is re-verified again during the manual purchase-verification step, not just once at listing time.

4

Property tax status

We confirm tax is paid and current — an unpaid tax history is one of the most common hidden problems in informal property deals.

This covers ownership and paperwork only. It is not a substitute for visiting the plot yourself — we tell every buyer this directly, on every listing.

Who's actually doing the checking

Fahim Islam — Documentation lead

Every listing is checked and signed off by a named person, not an automated stamp. You can message the person who verified a specific listing directly from that listing's page.

What protects your advance payment

Held, not transferred, until the contract is signed

Your advance payment is held by Terravue after you submit it — it is not sent to the seller until the sale contract is signed by both parties and final verification is complete. If verification fails at that stage, the full amount is refunded.

Every payment is tied to a specific order

Bank transfers, card payments and Bangla QR payments are all matched to your specific reference number (e.g. #TRV-8842) before they're counted as received — a payment sent without the right reference is flagged, not silently accepted.

This is not the same as an instant, automated purchase — that's deliberate. A property purchase this size should have a human checking it at every step, not just a payment confirmation screen.
